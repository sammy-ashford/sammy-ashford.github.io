About

I’m Sam Ashford. I shoot film and think about institutions and light in the same register.

This is where some of that ends up: Fox Point dusk, rooms that say more than the people in them, structures after their first life, and the small gaps where attention comes back online.

I am interested in dignity without performance. Weathered surfaces. Quiet labor. The way a relationship, a waterfront, or an old room can keep holding meaning after its original script has thinned out.

The photographs and essays here are not separate practices. They are both ways of staying with the evidence a little longer.
